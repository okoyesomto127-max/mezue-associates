Professional Practice

Our Professional Services

Disciplined property, valuation, management and consultancy solutions conducted in strict compliance with statutory regulations and recognized professional appraisal standards in Nigeria.

Core Valuation Discipline

Property Valuation

Professional valuation services for different types of property and property interests across residential, commercial, industrial, infrastructural, and agricultural sectors.

Mezue Associates delivers rigorous, statutory, and market-based valuation reports adhering strictly to recognized appraisal standards. Our valuation practice supports asset appraisal for financial reporting, loan security, insurance coverage, compulsory acquisition compensation, dispute settlement, and probate.

Key Valuation Mandates:

  • Asset valuation for balance sheet and accounting
  • Mortgage security appraisals for financial institutions
  • Compulsory acquisition & compensation claims
  • Capital gains, probate, and rating assessments
  • Plant, machinery, and equipment valuation
Asset Administration

Property Management

Management of properties with an emphasis on efficient administration and maintenance.

We oversee properties to optimize rental yields, maintain high occupancy levels, and preserve physical structural integrity. Our structured management framework provides transparent accounting, proactive lease renewals, and timely dispute resolution for individual and institutional owners.

Key Management Activities:

  • Rent collection and transparent disbursement schedules
  • Rigorous tenant vetting and lease documentation
  • Preventive maintenance and structural upkeep
  • Statutory charges & tenement rate reconciliation
Transactions & Brokering

Estate Agency

Professional assistance relating to property transactions, sales and lettings across Nigeria.

Our agency division connects qualified buyers, investors, and prospective tenants with vetted properties across prime commercial corridors and residential hubs throughout Nigeria. Every transaction is guided by thorough title verification, market-rate negotiations, and transparent procedural integrity.

Agency Services Include:

  • Commercial & residential sales representation
  • Corporate and individual lease brokering
  • Site acquisition for commercial developments
  • Transaction documentation coordination
Investment Appraisal

Feasibility & Viability Appraisal

Assessment of the feasibility and viability of property development proposals.

Before committing capital, developers and investors require clear, data-driven appraisals. Mezue Associates evaluates physical site characteristics, planning permissions, financial cash flow projections, sensitivity matrices, and return metrics to determine project viability.

Appraisal Deliverables:

  • Highest and best use (HBU) analysis
  • Financial projection modeling (NPV, IRR, Payback)
  • Market demand and supply risk assessment
  • Investor and bank financing documentation briefs
Operations & Infrastructure

Facility Management

Professional management and coordination of facilities and property-related operations.

We integrate physical workplace environments, plant machinery, security, and utility logistics to ensure seamless business continuity. Our facilities team implements service level agreements (SLAs), health and safety compliance, and energy-conscious operations.

Facility Focus:

  • Corporate office & multi-tenant building coordination
  • HVAC, power generator, and MEP maintenance management
  • Service charge budgeting and transparent audit reconciliation
Development Execution

Project Management

Property and development project coordination and management.

We safeguard owner interests across all phases of construction and refurbishment. Working closely with architects, structural engineers, and contractors, our project managers enforce timeline schedules, budget controls, and quality standards.

Coordination Scope:

  • Contractor procurement and contract administration
  • Milestone inspection and variation certification
  • Quality assurance and project handover commissioning
Strategic Advisory

Real Estate Consultancy

Professional advice relating to property, investment and real-estate matters.

Drawing on our founding in 1981 and decades of presence in the Nigerian market, our consultants provide strategic guidance for corporate balance sheets, institutional investors, estates, and private clients navigating changing market cycles.

Consultancy Focus:

  • Corporate property portfolio rationalization
  • Market trend analysis and location intelligence
  • Mortgage advisory and real-estate finance review
Tenure & Statutory Due Diligence

Property Rights Consultancy

Professional consultancy relating to acquisition, transfer and other property-right matters.

Land and property rights in Nigeria entail complex statutory, customary, and regulatory considerations. Mezue Associates provides expert guidance on titling pathways, certificates of occupancy, easements, covenants, and resolving tenure encumbrances.

Rights Advisory Areas:

  • Land title regularization and statutory documentation
  • Wayleaves, easements, and right-of-way negotiation
  • Tenure search coordination and boundary technical advisory

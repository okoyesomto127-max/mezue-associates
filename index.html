About Mezue Associates

A Firm of Estate Surveyors & Valuers Founded on Professionalism.

Established in 1981, Mezue Associates is an established Nigerian firm of Estate Surveyors & Valuers with extensive professional experience in property valuation, estate agency, property management, and corporate real estate consultancy.

Operating with a disciplined commitment to accuracy, statutory adherence, and market knowledge, the firm provides trusted property advisory for private individuals, corporate organizations, public institutions, and financial houses across Nigeria.

Professional Identity

Who We Are

Mezue Associates is an established Nigerian firm of Estate Surveyors & Valuers recognized for technical rigor, professional integrity, and institutional continuity.

As Estate Surveyors & Valuers registered under the Estate Surveyors and Valuers Registration Board of Nigeria (ESVARBON), our statutory mandate encompasses the comprehensive appraisal, management, development, and transactional administration of land and landed property interests across the country.

Our practice serves private property owners, financial institutions requiring reliable mortgage collateral valuations, corporate organizations optimizing real estate portfolios, and public-sector authorities executing infrastructure appraisals.

Institutional Heritage

Our History

Founded in 1981, Mezue Associates has maintained steady professional practice through four decades of Nigerian property evolution.

1981 — Practice Established in Enugu

In 1981, Mezue Associates commenced professional practice as a firm of Estate Surveyors & Valuers with its foundational headquarters located at 9 Ogui Road, Enugu.

Over the succeeding decades, as the Nigerian economy and property markets expanded into modern commercial corridors and emerging urban growth nodes, the firm expanded its operational capabilities while strictly preserving its core commitment to technical accuracy and professional valuation standards.

Today, the practice continues under experienced executive leadership, coordinating a network of active branches across key Nigerian economic locations from its historic Enugu base.
